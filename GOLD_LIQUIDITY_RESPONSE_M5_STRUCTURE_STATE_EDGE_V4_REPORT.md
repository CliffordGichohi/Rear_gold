## Verdict

`FAIL_SEMANTIC_REPRESENTATION`

V4 matched 5/16 human trade checkpoints (31.25%) against a frozen minimum of 10/16. It also produced eligible states on 11/14 NO_TRADE days (78.57%) against a frozen maximum of 7/14. Both gates failed.

## Interpretation

Liquidity-zone reaction plus an arbitrary M5 pivot break is not enough. Terminating on every opposite M5 pivot break also mistakes ordinary pullbacks for invalidation. The evidence requires both a more durable higher-timeframe invalidation rule and a point-in-time higher-timeframe location/direction discriminator.

No matched outcome, development outcome, 2025 value, 2026 value or PnL was opened. V4 receives zero economic credit and must not be retuned.
