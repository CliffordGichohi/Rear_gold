# Gold Matched Human Replay — H4 Structure Review V1

## Evidence status

This is a descriptive review of the already exposed January–February 2022 matched-human replay sample. It does not change any decision, outcome, verdict or sealed artifact and receives zero validation credit. Calendar 2025 and 2026 remain untouched.

Every assessment uses only completed H4 candles whose `available_at` timestamp was no later than the human decision timestamp. Confirmed two-sided fractal marks are supporting chart annotations, not an automatic trend rule. The final assessment follows the Reference Book's definitions: trend requires accepted progression through higher highs and higher lows or lower highs and lower lows; balance/range and structural transition are reported separately.

## Descriptive attribution

The eleven direction-right trades separate into two contextual families:

- Eight occurred within bullish outer structure or an active bullish H4 recovery: CBR-2022-002, 006, 013, 014, 015, 024, 027 and 030.
- Three were range/recovery responses rather than clean H4 trend continuations: CBR-2022-005, 020 and 023.

The five direction-wrong trades also separate coherently:

- CBR-2022-003 and 019 treated completed bearish H4 displacement and structural damage as an ordinary bullish retracement.
- CBR-2022-007, 009 and 026 entered long near an H4 upper boundary or after the bullish leg had already delivered most of its available range.

This is a post-result contextual attribution, not a prospective filter. Its value is identifying what the next fresh review must distinguish before outcome access: bullish trend phase, structural damage, range response and remaining room to the next opposing H4 reference level.

## Main conclusion

The operator was usually reading the broad bullish auction correctly, but the single label `bullish H4` compressed materially different states:

1. bullish impulse with room;
2. bullish pullback holding structure;
3. bullish but extended into an upper boundary;
4. balance/range response;
5. bearish structural transition after a formerly bullish regime.

Trend direction alone was therefore insufficient. The missing variables were the phase of the H4 auction, whether the last opposing swing had been accepted through, and how much unobstructed room remained before the next H4 decision area.
