# Gold All-Transition Auction Scanner Visualization Amendment A

## Reason

The sealed V1 event census and semantic certification remain unchanged.  The
original review charts drew every structural level across the complete panel,
which did not identify the candle that formed the referenced high or low.  The
charts were technically point-in-time correct but inadequate for human
semantic verification.

## Permitted correction

Replace only the review-atlas renderer:

- resolve every displayed level identity back to its causal confirmed swing;
- place a visible marker on the exact pivot candle and price;
- begin a short horizontal ray at that marker rather than at the panel edge;
- label the marker as consumed pivot, protected swing, unconsumed destination,
  known swing high, or known swing low;
- include the pivot timestamp in the label;
- expand the pre-decision window within a fixed cap when required to include a
  referenced pivot;
- omit a level from the plot, and report it explicitly, if its originating
  pivot cannot be shown without exceeding that cap.

No level may be drawn without a resolved source identity.  All candles must
remain completed and available no later than the event decision timestamp.

## Frozen boundaries

- Preserve all 729 event identities, fields, counts, hashes, classifications,
  and the original final seal.
- Preserve the 24 selected example identities and their order.
- Use the identical sealed primary and reference source streams.
- Render primary and reference independently and require byte-identical SVGs.
- Do not access outcomes, calculate performance, alter event semantics, add a
  trade filter, inspect fresh dates, acquire data, or incur a charge.

This is a visualization-only engineering amendment with zero research or
validation credit.
