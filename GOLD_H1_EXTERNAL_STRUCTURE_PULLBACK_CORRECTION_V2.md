## Frozen correction

1. H1 governing structure is built from external boundaries. Confirmed pivots
   that remain inside the current external high-low interval are internal and
   cannot by themselves reverse the governing direction. A new confirmed high
   strictly above the external high establishes LONG structure; a new confirmed
   low strictly below the external low establishes SHORT structure. Pivot and
   later confirmation timestamps remain separate.
2. The governing H1 control is the opposite external boundary known at the
   decision. Liquidity consumption and structure are separate facts: a local
   pivot being traded through does not silently replace the governing external
   boundary.
3. In a trend, M15 defines the active pullback leg using the latest confirmed
   direction-side terminal and the latest opposite pivot preceding it. The
   terminal need not remain unconsumed; taking a terminal is compatible with
   trend progress. Both pivots must have been confirmed before the decision.
4. The M5 trigger remains the frozen four-bar adverse-run/half-close turn,
   followed by the first valid retest within three completed M5 bars. Entry is
   still the retest close and the stop remains beyond the M5 turn/retest
   extreme plus the existing tick floor.
5. Trend targets remain the nearest pre-existing unconsumed H1 swing in the
   trade direction. The existing V1 range plan is carried unchanged; range
   logic is not modified here.
6. One plan is allowed per external-H1-state and M15-terminal identity. All
   timestamps and inputs must be point-in-time available.
