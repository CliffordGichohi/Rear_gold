## User diagram: `market instructs.svg`

The diagram has three directly usable instructions:

1. In an upward governing swing, inspect lower-timeframe price action at the
   pullback curvature and buy with that lower-timeframe action.
2. In a downward governing swing, inspect lower-timeframe price action at the
   rally curvature and sell with that lower-timeframe action.
3. Place invalidation beyond an internal lower-timeframe opposing swing and
   realize toward later opposing swing/liquidity references.

The lower panel also distinguishes consumed and unconsumed highs/lows. The
user's clarification governs: consumption requires later traded price strictly
beyond the exact pivot; equality is only engagement.

Frozen translation:

- environment is recorded before the entry;
- location must exist at the controlling curvature before confirmation;
- confirmation cannot create the location after price has already displaced;
- trend location uses the 0.705-0.886 retracement of the latest known impulse;
- M5 confirmation is a directional turn followed by the first adverse retest
  that fails at a better extreme and flips back in the intended direction;
- GEX or options-wall values are not invented when the sealed source does not
  contain them. Their state remains `UNKNOWN`, and their absence is not filled
  with a price-derived proxy.
- ordinary OHLC cannot be relabelled as observed delta or absorption. Those
  order-flow fields remain `UNKNOWN` outside existing GC coverage.

  footprint evidence are unavailable.

  match the operator's chart reading.
