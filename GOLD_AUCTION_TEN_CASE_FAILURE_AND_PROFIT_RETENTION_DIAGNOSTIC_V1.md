# Gold Auction Ten-Case Failure and Profit-Retention Diagnostic V1

Status: `FROZEN_EXPOSED_DIAGNOSTIC_PROTOCOL`

## Purpose and credit

Explain the already revealed outcomes of the ten frozen auction-placement
examples without changing their selection, direction, entry, stop, target, or
deadline. Separate setup/thesis failure from useful directional movement that
was not monetized, and measure one previously defined universal management
overlay.

This is a post-result diagnostic on a small, stratified, exposed population. It
has zero validation credit, cannot establish an edge, and cannot justify live
trading or monthly extrapolation.

## Population and sources

- Exactly the ten plan identities sealed by
  `GOLD_AUCTION_TRADE_PLACEMENT_EXAMPLES_V1`.
- Exactly their already revealed and sealed 2022 M1 outcome paths and existing
  M5 bars.
- No additional day, case, outcome, 2025, or 2026 source may be opened.
- No acquisition or charge is permitted.

## Frozen outcome attribution

Use the existing execution result and the existing execution MFE measured in
displayed structural-risk units:

1. `MONETIZED_SUCCESS`: net R50 is positive.
2. `DIRECTIONALLY_USEFUL_UNMONETIZED`: net R50 is non-positive and MFE is at
   least +1.00R.
3. `NO_MEANINGFUL_FAVOURABLE_AUCTION`: net R50 is non-positive and MFE is below
   +1.00R.

`DIRECTIONALLY_USEFUL` is the union of categories 1 and 2. These labels are
descriptive outcome labels, never decision-time features.

## Frozen pre-entry comparison registry

Extract only fields already present in each sealed plan:

- side, session, context family, event class;
- planned target room in R;
- signed macro alignment score (`direction_sign * macro_score`), macro
  confidence, state, and dominant driver;
- H1 and H4 completed-candle swing-relation pair and range location;
- decision distance from the M5 break in ATR;
- entry-to-stop risk divided by the frozen break ATR;
- target timeframe, protected-pivot timeframe, and local M15 liquidity room
  divided by entry-to-stop risk.

Report every case and group-level counts, means, medians, and categorical
distributions. With only ten exposed observations, calculate no p-values, fit
no classifier, create no veto, and promote no contrast into a trading rule.

## Frozen management overlay

Apply the previously registered `M5_CLOSE_PLUS_1R_NET_BREAK_EVEN` identity to
all ten trades, not only losses:

- preserve the original fill, structural stop, absolute target, deadline,
  costs, quantity, and stop-first ambiguity convention;
- arm only after the first completed M5 close at or beyond +1.00 effective
  structural R from the actual fill;
- from that timestamp, use the established cost-adjusted net-break-even level;
- if the level is touched before the unchanged target, resolve the trade at
  exactly 0.00R50; if target and protection are touched in one M1 bar, apply
  protection first;
- if the target resolved before activation, or protection was never touched,
  leave the baseline result unchanged.

The cost allowance is frozen as the fill-bar spread plus two times the existing
$0.05-per-ounce slippage assumption, matching the previously registered
day-by-day overlay identity. Report activations, saved losses, clipped winners,
unchanged trades, incremental R50, profit factor, and drawdown.

## Integrity and stopping rules

- Freeze protocol, implementation, tests, exact plan identities, and all input
  hashes before calculating the diagnostic.
- Prove long/short symmetry, completed-M5 activation, target precedence, and
  stop-first protection on synthetic bars before reading the ten paths.
- Run the sealed primary and reference sources separately and require identical
  plans, attributions, overlays, results, summaries, and checksums.
- Do not change thresholds, test an alternative overlay, create an entry
  filter, or open fresh data after seeing the result.
- Record the achieved exposed diagnostic honestly and stop.
